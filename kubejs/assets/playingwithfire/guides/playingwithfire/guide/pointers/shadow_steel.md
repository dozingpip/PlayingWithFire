# Shadow Steel

Confused by the term 'Mysterious Conversion'? It's different for every item that uses that term... For shadow steel that means dropping your chromatic alloy into the void. Upon entering the void, it will not be destroyed, instead it will levitate back up to the height you threw it from and stay there until you pick it up.
