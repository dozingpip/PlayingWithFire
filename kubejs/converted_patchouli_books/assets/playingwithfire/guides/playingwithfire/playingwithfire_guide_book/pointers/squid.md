# Squid and Glow Squid

<ItemImage id="minecraft:ink_sac" />

There is a method to summon squid to this hell. A glow squid will spawn if you right click a honey block with a cyan glimmering flower. Squids don't suffocate in liquid honey. Once you have a glow squid you can use black dye on it to transform it into a normal squid.
